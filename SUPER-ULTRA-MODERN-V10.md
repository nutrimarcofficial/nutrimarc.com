# NutriMarc Super Ultra Modern V10

V10 strengthens the NutriMarc Group architecture by positioning NHFS and NAIS as two distinct, equally important units immediately after the hero. It adds an animated unit gateway, premium dark emerald visual system, advanced CSS/SVG-style node/orbit graphics, responsive unit cards, and preserves the existing storefront, cart, coupon, checkout, bootcamp registration, CSR, enquiry, and backend structure.
